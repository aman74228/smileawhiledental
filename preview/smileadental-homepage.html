Advanced technology

Advanced technology for precise, comfortable care

We invest in modern, low-radiation equipment so every diagnosis is clearer, every treatment is gentler, and you spend less time in the chair.

01 / 08
  1. 01

    3D CBCT scanning

    Detailed 3D images of teeth, bone and nerves for safer implant planning and precise diagnosis.

  2. 02

    Digital radiography

    Instant, high-resolution X-rays with much lower radiation than traditional film.

  3. 03

    3D intraoral scanner

    Fast, comfortable digital impressions, with no messy trays or gagging.

  4. 04

    Intraoral camera

    See exactly what we see, on screen, so every treatment decision is clear.

  5. 05

    CEREC same-day crowns

    Crowns designed, milled and fitted in a single visit, with no temporaries.

  6. 06

    Soft-tissue dental laser

    Gentler gum treatments with less bleeding, fewer stitches and faster healing.

  7. 07

    Digital smile design

    Preview your new smile before treatment begins, and shape it together with us.

  8. 08

    Computer-guided anesthesia

    Precise, steady numbing for a far more comfortable injection.
