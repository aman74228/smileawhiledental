Design system v0.1

Calm, precise, quietly confident.

The foundation for the Smileawhile Dental redesign: a slate-and-teal palette taken directly from the current brand, one variable typeface doing the heavy lifting, and motion that only moves when it means something.

01

Why the references feel premium

Measured from the live pages (computed styles at 1440px), not from memory. The common thread is restraint: one typeface, very few colors, huge type contrast and generous space doing the work that effects usually try to do.

Ivora Dental

ivoradental.framer.website
  • One family, one weight for headings. Switzer 500 everywhere, from 20px to 88px. Hierarchy comes from size alone, so nothing competes.
  • Tracking scales with size. −0.05em at 88px, −0.04em at 54px, −0.025em at 30px, ~0 at body. That's why large type looks "set" rather than typed.
  • Color is ~90% neutral. A near-black ink (#0A1020), cool off-whites (#EDF1F6 / #F4F7FB) and a single blue (#2347E8) reserved for CTAs and a few highlighted words.
  • Pills plus a 20–28px card radius, with shadows that are almost invisible: a 1px inner white highlight plus a tinted −12px spread. Depth without "drop shadow".
  • Brand-voice statement sections. The paragraph-as-headline ("We believe great dentistry should feel calm…") with grey text and the key phrase in brand color. Confidence through copy, not decoration.

H1 88/88 −4.4px · H2 54/56 −2.16px · H3 30/34.5 −0.75px · Body 18/27.9 #566174 · Radii 999 / 28 / 20 / 14

Makro

makro.framer.website
  • Regular-weight display type. Inter Display 400 at 88px and 56px with −0.04em tracking. Lighter weight at large sizes reads as calm and expensive.
  • Generous line-height on display (1.25 at 88px). Headlines breathe instead of stacking tightly.
  • Text opacity instead of more grey tokens. Body is ink at 75%, so every grey stays the exact same hue as the ink.
  • A small, repeated radius vocabulary (12 / 16 / 24 + pill). Every surface feels like it came from one system.
  • One loud accent (lime #D9FF5C) used in tiny doses on a white/cool-grey base. Proof that restraint makes the accent work harder.

H1 88/110 −3.52px · H2 56/67 −2.24px · H4 32/46 −0.96px · Body 18/27 @75% · Radii 12 / 24 / 16 / pill

Peridot

peridotapp.framer.website
  • Editorial scale jumps. Section titles at 140–150px against 18px body. The contrast in size itself is the design.
  • Very tight tracking at display (−0.05em to −0.06em) with line-height ≤ 1.0, so headings read as a single shape.
  • Warm-neutral base (#F3F5EE / #EEF1E6) with one brand tint (#D9F27A) and near-black ink, used as atmospheric gradient washes, never as flat fills.
  • Big rounded image masks (28–40px) with consistent photo grading, so a mixed photo set still looks art-directed.
  • Soft, long, tinted shadows (0 40px 70px at 22%, green-tinted) only under hero devices. One depth moment per screen.

H2 150/150 −7.5px · H2 120/114 −6px · H3 40/44 −0.8px · Radii 28 / 36 / 40 / pill · Shadows tinted, 10–22%

What we take

  • Size-only hierarchy, one weight (500) for all headings
  • Tracking that tightens as size grows (−0.01 → −0.035em)
  • 90% neutral surfaces, brand teal only for action and two hero moments
  • One radius vocabulary (16 / 20 / 24 / 28 / pill) and tinted, near-invisible shadows
  • Statement paragraphs with a highlighted key phrase

What we leave behind

  • Ivora's 3D tooth hero and "scroll to clean" gimmick (explicitly banned)
  • Peridot's bento-grid feature wall and lifestyle stock models
  • 150px editorial headings. Too loud for a healthcare audience; we cap display at 104px.
  • Regular-weight display (Makro). Google Sans has no Light, and 400 at 100px loses authority, so we use 500.

Glass icon set

One custom set, drawn on a 32px grid: the six service categories first, with the eight technology icons to follow in Phase 3 using the same rules. Shown on a tinted mesh so the frosted tile is visible.

Emergency Dentistrygi-emergency
Dental Implantsgi-implants

General Dentistrygi-general
Cosmeticgi-cosmetic

Geometry32×32 grid, 2px safe area. 1.5px stroke (non-scaling), round caps and joins. Rendered at 32px in a 56px tile, or 42px in a 72px tile (≈ 58% fill).
MaterialDiagonal fill teal-25 → #B9E3EF → #4FBCD6. Stroke teal-800. A white 1.5px highlight arc sits top-left on every primary shape, so light always comes from the top-left.
TileWhite 85→40% glass, 14px backdrop blur, 1px white border plus an 8% navy hairline (white/20% alone disappears on white backgrounds). On hover, the tile lifts 2px and picks up a teal ring.

Grid & containers

10

Motion system

One house curve, expo.out (CSS cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)), for everything that enters. Things arrive fast and settle slowly. Only transform and opacity are animated; with reduced motion, all transforms are dropped and only opacity fades remain.
